Amenities

Everything You Need, Included

Our properties come packed with amenities designed for the student lifestyle. From study spaces to fitness centers, we have it all covered.

High-Speed WiFi

Gigabit fiber internet included in every unit. Stream, study, and stay connected without interruptions.

Covered Parking

Secure, covered parking spaces available for all residents. No more searching for a spot before class.

Fitness Center

24/7 access to a fully equipped gym with cardio machines, free weights, and yoga studio.

Pet Friendly

Bring your furry friends along. Select properties feature dog parks and pet washing stations.

24/7 Security

Controlled access, security cameras, and on-call staff ensure you feel safe at all times.

Utilities Included

Water, electricity, and trash service bundled into your rent for one predictable monthly payment.

In-Unit Laundry

Washer and dryer in each unit so you can do laundry on your own schedule, no quarters needed.

Swimming Pool

Resort-style pool and sun deck perfect for weekends and unwinding after exams.

Study Lounges

Quiet study rooms and co-working spaces with whiteboards, printers, and charging stations.

Community Kitchen

Shared gourmet kitchen for hosting dinner parties, baking, or just making a late-night snack.

Bike Storage

Secure indoor bike racks and repair stations for cyclists who prefer two wheels to campus.

Green Spaces

Landscaped courtyards, rooftop gardens, and outdoor seating areas to relax between classes.
